# Support opencode-vscode

opencode-vscode is developed in the open, pre-1.0, by a single maintainer. If
opencode-vscode saves you time, a small donation helps cover the cost of the
time, hosting and coffee that go into it. Every contribution is genuinely
appreciated.

Donations are received directly to the maintainer's Binance account via
crypto. Only the two networks below are accepted — please do not send anything
else.

## BTC (Bitcoin)

```

```

- **Network:** Bitcoin (native). Minimum deposit: `0.000005 BTC`.
- No memo/tag required.

## USDT (TRC-20, Tron)

```
TManz28TdkVdepxRaEaS1EWJytGaQ9W4QT
```

- **Network:** TRON (TRC-20). Minimum deposit: `0.01 USDT`.
- No memo/tag required.
- TRC-20 is recommended for USDT: fees are usually fractions of a cent and
  transfers settle in under two minutes, which is why most senders pick it.

## How to send

1. Make sure **the coin and the network match exactly** what is written above
   (BTC on Bitcoin; USDT on TRON/TRC-20).
2. Confirm the amount is above the minimum for the network.
3. Send a small test amount the first time; once it lands, you can send the
   rest.
4. Keep the transaction ID (TXID) until the transfer is credited.

## Please read this

- **A wrong network means lost funds.** Sending USDT via ERC-20 (Ethereum) or
  BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain), or BTC via any other chain, sends the money to an
  address Binance cannot credit — it is not recoverable. Only the two networks
  listed above are accepted.
- **No one will ever ask you for more.** The maintainer will never ask for your
  account details, email, passwords or private keys. Only ever send to the two
  addresses above.
- Donations are optional and do not buy anything: opencode-vscode stays free
  and open-source under the MIT license regardless.
